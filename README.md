A 100-word monologue spoken by Malachar, dark lord of the Drowned Causeway — a villain with a scheme (flood the valley, crown the raven) and a history (the tax collector, the dragon, his mother all ahead of you in line), who is secretly just very tired. The tiredness lives in the rhythm, the pauses and the small deflections — the cold throne, the screaming hinges, the kettle older than your kingdom — never in a plain statement that he is weary. It ends on a sting: the dark lord merely... pauses. Between conquests. Forever, if the kettle holds out.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

`main.ts` writes the monologue to `/output/entry.txt` (exactly 100 words).
